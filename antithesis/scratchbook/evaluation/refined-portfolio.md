## Antithesis fit

handoff, Brady isolation and backup publication rows have fault, race or

## Wildcard: composition and claim boundaries

The strongest remaining risk is a favorable local result being promoted to a
broader customer or physical claim. `authoritative-dns-follows-current-placement`
illustrates this directly. A current A record, a current placement row and a
successful TCP handshake do not independently show that the destination guest
serves the retained customer's bytes. The composed create and stopped-move
evidence require exact VM, boot, UID, disk and route correlation. Preserve those
joins when assigning templates and variants. In particular, TCG guest success
cannot prove Firecracker jail/cgroup, and a single outer VM cannot prove two
independent host failures. A clock-fault request cannot count as a reached
backward clock step in backup publication; the manifest must be observed through
real Restic publication and its retention validator.
